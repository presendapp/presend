Why use a browser-based tool?

Since iOS 11, iPhones save photos in HEIC format instead of JPG. While this saves storage space, Windows 10 and 11 cannot open HEIC files without installing a paid codec extension from the Microsoft Store. When you receive iPhone photos via email, AirDrop, or messaging apps on your PC, you often see a blank image or an error message. Our HEIC to JPG Converter decodes your iPhone photos entirely in your browser — no download, no install, no codec needed. Your photos are processed locally and converted to JPG files that open on any Windows PC, Android phone, website, or email client.

How it works

1. Upload your HEIC file — drag and drop from your iPhone or PC.
2. Preview the result — verify the converted image looks correct.
3. Download the JPG — opens instantly on Windows, Mac, Android, and web.
