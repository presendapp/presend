Why count words and characters?

Word count is the invisible backbone of professional writing. Journalists need to hit exact lengths for print columns (800 words) or online articles (1500 words). Students face strict essay limits — too short loses marks, too long gets rejected. SEO writers optimize blog posts between 1500-2500 words for Google ranking. Social media managers stay under Twitter's 280 characters or LinkedIn's 3000-character limit. Translators charge per word, so accurate counts directly affect invoices. Our Word Counter goes beyond simple word count: it tracks characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, and estimates reading time based on average adult speed (200 words per minute). It works in any language — English, French, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi — using Unicode-aware boundaries. The tool updates in real time as you type, with no upload, no server, and no data exposure. Perfect for essays, articles, social media posts, translations, and any text where precision matters.
