Presend vs the alternatives

An honest look at how Presend compares to the most popular online file tools.

Why compare?

Most online tools do the same thing: upload your file to a server, process it, then send it back. Presend does everything inside your browser. Your files never leave your device. Here's how that changes the trade-offs.

When to use Presend

  • You handle sensitive photos or documents (journalism, activism, legal, medical)
  • You don't trust third-party servers with your files
  • You want to verify the code is doing what it claims (open source)
  • You need to work offline or on a slow connection

When to use the others

  • You need batch processing of hundreds of files
  • You need advanced features like OCR, compression, or format conversion
  • You don't mind uploading files to a reputable service
