How to Clean URLs Before Sharing on Social Media

Links copied from social media, newsletters, or ads are often full of tracking parameters that tell the recipient's platform where the link came from. Here's how to strip them.

What are tracking parameters?

UTM tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign), Facebook click IDs (fbclid), Google click IDs (gclid), and dozens of other parameters are appended to URLs to track who clicked what. They make links long, ugly, and invasive.

Why remove them?

Clean links look more professional, don't leak your browsing context, and won't break if the tracking service changes. They're also shorter and easier to share in messages with character limits.

How to clean a URL

Paste the messy link into our URL Cleaner. It automatically detects and removes 50+ known tracking parameters while preserving the actual destination. Nothing is sent to any server.

Does it affect affiliate links?

If you need to keep certain parameters (like affiliate IDs), you can review what was removed before copying the clean link. The tool shows you exactly what it stripped.
